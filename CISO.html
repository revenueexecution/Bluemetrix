The AI data security gap

Traditional discovery controls are highly effective at finding recognised sensitive-data types such as PII, PHI, payment data and known patterns.

But they may have no reason to recognise that an internal product code, ingredient combination, project name, process description, technical phrase or formulation is commercially sensitive.

Can we identify what is sensitive to our organisation before that information is exposed to AI?

When to consider this capability

  • Employees or applications are sending enterprise data to AI systems.
  • Standard PII/PHI detection does not cover the organisation’s full definition of sensitive information.
  • The business wants to move AI from pilot to production using real operational data.
  • Security needs a practical control that does not simply block the AI use case.
  • Proprietary terminology, technical language or internal codes carry sensitivity that generic controls may not understand.
  • The organisation needs to preserve data utility while reducing exposure.
